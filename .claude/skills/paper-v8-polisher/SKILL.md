# Paper V8 Polisher

Use the supplied source in `docs/source/PAPER-V8-POLISHER-INPUT.md` for detailed workflow requirements when revising a paper. Diagnose the strongest and weakest sections, then make focused revisions to the abstract, contribution, methods, discussion, limitations, conclusion and figure captions.

Preserve the author's argument, uncertainty and distinctive framework. Separate sourced findings from inference; never fabricate citations, archival support, novelty scores, reviewer comments or evidence of publication. Verify sources before adding citations. Discuss policy relevance only when supported by the scholarship, without concealing material facts or promising any legal outcome.

Return a concise assessment, prioritized revisions, actual edited text, sensitive-claim notes, figure suggestions and an honest readiness verdict. No journal submission, social promotion or fee is authorized merely by requesting a revision. Keep private draft/petition content out of the public NEO repository.
